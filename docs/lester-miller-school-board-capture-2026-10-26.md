Lester M. Miller represented **District 4 on the Bibb County Board of Education
from January 2013 through December 2020**, two four-year terms: 2013–2016 and
2017–2020. The collection window is January 1, 2013–December 31, 2020, in
America/New_York. Those are term boundaries, not an assertion that his first oath
was administered on January 1.

## Listing coverage and the early 2013 gap

The [official Simbli listing](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/SB_Meetings/SB_MeetingListing.aspx?S=4013)
and its constrained read-only archive pagination were exhausted: **21 pages and
1,015 distinct all-time rows**. Source responses/request provenance are retained
privately. Discovery run: `599bb69e-b877-442c-bdae-8bd76dc747e0`.

The 306 in-scope public entries comprise 192 Board Meetings, 88 Committee
Meetings, 24 Work Sessions and two Training Meetings. Called meetings categorized
as Board Meetings are included. The training entries are explicitly whole-board
governance training. School councils and the ESPLOST citizen oversight committee
are outside this board-level capture; their public listing metadata is preserved
separately. A listing does not establish Miller's attendance.

**Simbli's oldest listing is October 17, 2013.** Earlier meeting bundles cannot
be inventoried completely from this archive. A low 2013 listing count must not
be interpreted as evidence that earlier meetings did not occur.

Two formal minutes PDFs attached to the October 17 board agenda recover three
earlier dated occurrences: the **September 19 regular meeting** and the
**September 20–21 retreat**. Each date explicitly lists Miller as present.
Announced, called-to-order and adjournment times, locations, PDF hashes/pages
and original attachment provenance are saved in `recovered-meetings.json` /
`.csv`, with searchable text in `minutes-text/`. The PDF pages supporting
attendance and opening times were also checked visually. These occurrences
have no standalone listing IDs in the current archive; no official IDs were
invented. Their two PDFs are already included in the document counts above.

September 19's PDF explicitly says it was approved on October 17. The retreat
PDF does not itself state an approval date. September 19's confirmation section
also documents **August 15, August 20 and September 11** meetings, but those full
minutes have not been located. `prior-meeting-references.json` preserves these
references without inferring Miller's attendance.

## Supplementary newsletter archive

older search-located issues: nine from 2013, one from 2014 and six from 2015.

These are district summaries and remain distinct from formal minutes.

Eight 2013 newsletter dates predate the Simbli listing: January 17; March 14;
April 8, 11, 16 and 18; and May 16 and 21. They provide additional evidence of
earlier meetings but do not close the historical gap. Search results do not
establish that every older issue was found.

`brief-index.json` preserves explicit opening-summary meeting dates where
available; email forwarding/publication dates are not treated as meeting dates.
Thirteen newsletters match Miller's full name. `brief-linked-resources.json`
inventories **1,012 PDF hyperlinks**; their targets have not been downloaded.

## Validation and collector changes

Offline replay in an isolated SQLite backup reproduced all **seven 2013 and 52
2014 records with zero new proposals**. The 2014 replay correctly retains the
two source-gap errors; it does not relabel an incomplete capture as complete.
Every exported complete/partial document and all 104 original newsletters were
checked against their recorded digests and sizes. No collector remained active
when the paused snapshot was verified.

Collector changes committed locally:

- `d1d6dde`: support historical null/empty filenames through explicit supported FileExtension; include whole-board training.
- `acf7480`: retain other verified public resources after an individual document returns HTML, while withholding the incomplete bundle.
- `30f9b86`: after human verification, recognize the narrow empty official attachment form as a terminal response so the browser does not wait for a nonexistent file. The HTML still fails document validation.

**175 tests passed**, including eight browser cases. Regression failures were
demonstrated before fixes. Ruff, Bandit and whitespace checks passed. Synthetic
browser fixtures exercise verification plumbing; no real CAPTCHA is solved
automatically.

The original long process ended while waiting for verification; its saved
checkpoints were resumed. Subsequent work uses one bounded process per annual
pass through `collect_slice.py`, with 1,000 requests per pass (below the 2,000
systematic ceiling), 30 listing pages, 100 meetings, 5,000 resources, 500 MB per
response, 10 GB per pass and 180 seconds per response. One-second pacing,
server deferrals, persisted cooldowns, five recovery targets per run, 45 browser
attempts/hour, public-destination/TLS checks and visibility filters remain.
Unknown HTML and challenges do not receive the empty-form exception.
